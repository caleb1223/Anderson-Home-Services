Services

Furniture assembly, move-in setup & light home help.

Based in Arlington, I help clients across Washington DC, Northern Virginia, and Montgomery County, MD assemble furniture and get settled after a move. From a single item to a full apartment furniture order, send the complete list so I can plan the time and scope together.

Full Day Package — $750 Flat Rate

Best for large projects where you want one predictable day and one predictable price: move-ins, multiple rooms, full furniture orders, or a long list of home setup tasks. This blocks the day for your project and can run well into the evening when needed. Requires 50% deposit.

I mount TVs on drywall walls only. Send the TV size, mount details, and a photo of the wall so I can review the setup before booking. Plaster, brick, concrete, and ceiling mounts are outside my scope.

Light Home Work

Picture hanging on standard walls, small furniture fixes, basic IT setup, yard help, and other practical tasks. Include these in your project list and I’ll let you know what I can handle.

🌙 Night Owl · $10/hr off

Receive $10/hr off hourly work performed from 8pm onward, even if the appointment starts earlier. For example, a 7–9pm job is $60 for 7–8pm and $50 for 8–9pm: $110 in labor before any other discount.

🕒 Flex Start · $10/hr off

  1. I choose a time approximately 2 weeks out.
  2. I may keep that time or adjust it; if I adjust it I will provide at least 24 hours’ notice.
  3. You receive $10/hr off either way.

Prefer not to start at night? You can opt out of an 8pm-or-later Flex Start. Otherwise, evening starts may be likely when they help fill a gap in my schedule.

Stack them: On a Flex Start appointment, the rate is $50/hr before 8pm and $40/hr for time worked from 8pm onward. Night Owl applies to the evening portion, not the entire appointment.
