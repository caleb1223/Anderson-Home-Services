About Anderson Home Services

Furniture assembly, move-in setup, and practical home help.

Hi, I’m Caleb. Since 2022, I’ve assembled thousands of pieces of furniture across the DMV — everything from single nightstands to large wardrobe systems, garden sheds, and full furnishing of homes.

My goal is simple: leave you with a complete home; not a half-built dresser in the corner or a living room full of deliveries.

I’m not a giant company or a polished concierge service. I’m a local operator who takes the work seriously, communicates clearly, and tries to make the process easier than expected. View my LinkedIn.
