Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

If something isn't answered here, just reach out directly.

Are you insured? ▾
Yes! I carry business liability insurance with $1 million in coverage. My live insurance certificate is available to view here.
How do I book if I'm unsure on timing? ▾
No worries — just contact me directly. I'll tell you availability and give you a time estimate so you can pick the right slot.
What's your policy on start times or running late? ▾
I do my best to arrive on time — I overestimate job lengths and build buffer windows between bookings. I'm more likely to run early than late. That said, like any professional visiting multiple homes in a day, delays can happen. I work with 30-minute arrival windows, and I extend that same courtesy to clients. If I give a 5–5:30pm window and you're stuck in traffic until 5:30, your start time is 5:30 with no extra charge.
My situation requires certainty on timing. What are my options? ▾
A couple options: (1) Be my first job of the day at 10:30–11am — this eliminates the risk of earlier jobs running long. (2) Book at night, especially after 8pm — night bookings are less common, so there's far less chance of any cascade delay.
What if you can't complete something on-site? ▾
If I'm unable to complete an agreed-upon task once I'm there — for example, a mount I can't do or WiFi equipment I can't configure — you pay nothing for that time. I'll also try to connect you with someone who can.
Will you travel beyond the DC/NoVA area? ▾
Depending on project scope, yes. Jobs beyond the DMV area may have a larger hourly minimum and/or require a deposit. Just reach out and we'll figure it out.
Do you charge a deposit or cancellation fee? ▾
For most smaller jobs, no. For larger jobs I may ask for a deposit, since filling a full day slot after a late cancellation is difficult. That deposit counts toward your final price. I'm always willing to work with you on reschedules and genuine emergencies.
What do I need to know about the Full Day Package? ▾
The Full Day Package is the most reliable option for large move-ins. Because I block my entire day for you, I require a 50% deposit. If you cancel less than 48 hours before the start time, that deposit is retained. For genuine emergencies or rescheduling needs, contact me — I'll do my best to work something out.
What's the fastest way to reach you? ▾
Texting is fastest. WhatsApp and email also work. I try to limit phone use during active jobs, so please give me a few hours — and if you haven't heard back, feel free to follow up.
What about hauling — what can you take? ▾
My vehicle is a 2003 Suburban, so most furniture fits — especially pre-assembly. I can haul project trash like boxes and old furniture, but not hazardous materials or general household waste. I'm also open to renting a box truck or pickup on a case-by-case basis.
What can't you do? ▾
Most projects are within my skillset, but some exceptions: complex mounts (TV wall mounts, ceiling mounts, mounts to plaster or concrete), wall-mounted Murphy beds, and anything requiring a license — like most electrical or plumbing work. If you're unsure, just ask. I'll tell you honestly and try to refer you to someone who can help.
